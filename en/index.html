Frequently asked questions

Why reply to Google reviews?

When choosing a company, people read not only the reviews but also how the company replied. An unanswered review, especially a negative one, looks ignored.

Is it safe to add you to our Google Business Profile?

Manager rights let us reply to reviews, but not delete the profile or remove the owner. We never change your profile details unless you ask, and you can remove our access at any time.

Can you delete negative reviews?

No. We never delete, write, buy or filter reviews, because that breaks Google's rules. The best answer to a negative review is a calm, quick reply.

Which languages do you reply in?

We reply to Lithuanian reviews in Lithuanian, and to reviews in any other language in English.

Do you guarantee a better rating?

No, your rating depends on your customers and on Google. We guarantee that every new review gets a reply within 48 hours, and you get a report every month.
