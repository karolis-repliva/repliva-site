Privacy note

Updated: 12 October 2026

Who we are

The data controller is Karolis Kilijonas, brand name "Repliva.lt". Contact: karolis@repliva.lt. We offer companies a service that replies to their Google reviews.

What data we handle

  • Work contact details of company staff: name (where published), work email, job title, company name and code.
  • The content and dates of our correspondence.
  • Public Google reviews about the company (the review text and the reviewer's name as Google shows it), when we prepare sample replies.

Where it comes from

Company websites, public registers (the Centre of Registers), business directories and the company's Google Business Profile.

Why, and on what basis

To offer our service to a company that may find it useful. The basis is our legitimate interest (GDPR Article 6(1)(f)). We write only to legal persons, as the Lithuanian Law on Electronic Communications allows, and every email lets you opt out.

Who receives it

  • Google (Google Workspace), for email and documents.
  • An AI writing tool (Anthropic) receives only the company name and public review text, to draft the email and sample replies. It never receives contact details or reviewers' names. A person checks every text before it is sent.
  • We do not sell or share the data with anyone else.

Transfers outside the EU

These providers may process data outside the European Economic Area, using the safeguards GDPR provides for (such as standard contractual clauses).

How long we keep it

If you do not reply, we delete the data within 6 months of our last email. If you opt out, we keep only the email address, company and date, so that we never write again. If you become a client, the contract sets how long we keep data.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your data, or limit how we use it. You can object to our emails at any time: reply "UNSUBSCRIBE" or write to karolis@repliva.lt. We answer within 30 days. You can complain to the State Data Protection Inspectorate (vdai.lrv.lt).

This website

This website uses no cookies, analytics or contact forms. Fonts load from Google Fonts, so your browser connects to Google's servers.
